Files read: 01, 02 + full catalog, 03-10
Working assumption: [H] Shop.io sells a hosted commerce platform (checkout, B2C, B2B and retail, plus migration support) to brands up to enterprise category leaders; best fit where a brand's online stack slows growth or splits across brands and markets. Taken from shop.io/enterprise, read today; correct it and I will rebuild on your version.
TARGET read as: Whitfield & Co., the Philadelphia jewelry and watch retailer, wholly owned by Calder Brands (Nasdaq-listed). Not Whitfield Capital, the Ohio debt-collection company, which dominates search results for the same name.
Meeting: Daniel Reyes, Director of E-commerce, Whitfield · Prepared: Oct 27, 2026 · Seller: Shop.io · History: none given

Meeting card

The bet: Since January the group runs two jewelry brands online, and its own Q2 report says Marlowe stays fully separate for the customer while the group starts sharing what the customer never sees: online sales, IT and data. Marlowe sells online at a far higher rate than Whitfield, and the man who runs Whitfield's online is the obvious source of that know-how.

Ask (menu, not script):

  1. Which parts of Marlowe's online have landed on your desk since January?holding: the Q2 report lists online sales, IT and data among the areas where group know-how goes to Marlowe
  2. What has to stay separate between the two shops, and what can be shared?holding: the same report keeps Marlowe's online fully separate for the customer and puts synergies only where the customer cannot see them
  3. What will Marlowe's online need as the new boutiques open?holding: the plan is five to ten new Marlowe shops a year, and each boutique has its own engraving atelier

Watch out: Do not bring up Marlowe's old UK storefront, which ran on Shop.io in 2025 and is switched off today, until you have checked Shop.io's own records on that account. If he raises it, ask what happened and listen.

Timing: THIS WEEK, on a soft signal: his jury seat at the Commerce Leader of the Year gala eleven days ago. Next catalyst: Calder's Q3 report on November 24; its quiet period ends the same day, so no unpublished numbers. Budget year is the calendar year, so you are in the quarter where next year's lines get set.

Account X-ray compiles the facts. Then it hands you the angles.

The facts

Know what's true before you say it

The money, what just changed, who decides, and the tool they already run. Every line carries its source and date, drawn from 39 signals across filings, hiring, press and tech changes.

The POVs

Five to seven angles to try

Points of view built from the facts it found, in a suggested order, with the risky ones flagged. Ideas to take into the meeting, not scripts.

The self-check

It says what it couldn't check

You could do this three other ways.

Doing it yourself across ten tabs

It takes long enough that you only do it for the big logos and wing the rest.

Pasting the company name into ChatGPT

Even with search on, it skims a page or two, dates nothing, and blends what it found with what it remembers. You cannot tell which line is real.

Sales intelligence platforms

Firmographics, intent scores and a seat licence. None of it tells you what to say in minute two.
